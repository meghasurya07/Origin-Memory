## 1. Foundational Neuroscience

### Memory Systems & Hippocampus

1. **Ebbinghaus, H.** (1885). *Memory: A Contribution to Experimental Psychology*. — The original forgetting curve experiments
2. **Miller, G.A.** (1956). "The Magical Number Seven, Plus or Minus Two: Some Limits on Our Capacity for Processing Information." *Psychological Review*, 63(2), 81-97. — Working memory capacity limits
3. **Craik, F.I.M. & Lockhart, R.S.** (1972). "Levels of Processing: A Framework for Memory Research." *Journal of Verbal Learning and Verbal Behavior*, 11(6), 671-684. — Depth of processing theory
4. **Cowan, N.** (2001). "The Magical Number 4 in Short-Term Memory: A Reconsideration of Mental Storage Capacity." *Behavioral and Brain Sciences*, 24(1), 87-114. — Updated capacity estimates (4 ± 1)
5. **Squire, L.R.** (2004). "Memory Systems of the Brain: A Brief History and Current Perspective." *Neurobiology of Learning and Memory*, 82(3), 171-177. — Memory systems taxonomy
6. **Tonegawa, S., Pignatelli, M., Roy, D.S., & Ryan, T.J.** (2015). "Memory Engram Storage and Retrieval." *Current Opinion in Neurobiology*, 35, 101-109. — Modern engram research
7. **Oxford University** (2026). "Brief, slow rhythms in the hippocampus coordinate memory-related brain regions." — Rhythmic coordination breakthrough

### Complementary Learning Systems

8. **McClelland, J.L., McNaughton, B.L., & O'Reilly, R.C.** (1995). "Why There Are Complementary Learning Systems in the Hippocampus and Neocortex: Insights from the Successes and Failures of Connectionist Models of Learning and Memory." *Psychological Review*, 102(3), 419-457. — The foundational CLS theory paper
9. **Kumaran, D., Hassabis, D., & McClelland, J.L.** (2016). "What Learning Systems Do Intelligent Agents Need? Complementary Learning Systems Theory Updated." *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, 20(7), 512-534. — CLS theory updated for the deep learning era

### Synaptic Plasticity & Learning Rules

10. **Hebb, D.O.** (1949). *The Organization of Behavior*. — "Neurons that fire together, wire together"
11. **Bliss, T.V.P. & Lømo, T.** (1973). "Long-lasting potentiation of synaptic transmission in the dentate area of the anaesthetized rabbit following stimulation of the perforant path." *Journal of Physiology*, 232(2), 331-356. — Discovery of LTP
12. **Bienenstock, E.L., Cooper, L.N., & Munro, P.W.** (1982). "Theory for the development of neuron selectivity: orientation specificity and binocular interaction in visual cortex." *Journal of Neuroscience*, 2(1), 32-48. — BCM learning rule
13. **Oja, E.** (1982). "Simplified neuron model as a principal component analyzer." *Journal of Mathematical Biology*, 15(3), 267-273. — Normalized Hebbian learning
14. **Markram, H., Lübke, J., Frotscher, M., & Sakmann, B.** (1997). "Regulation of Synaptic Efficacy by Coincidence of Postsynaptic APs and EPSPs." *Science*, 275(5297), 213-215. — STDP discovery
15. **Wixted, J.T. & Ebbesen, E.B.** (1991). "On the form of forgetting." *Psychological Science*, 2(6), 409-415. — Power law of forgetting

## 7. Recommended Reading Order

For someone new to this space:

1. Start with **McClelland et al. (1995)** [#8] — The CLS theory is the foundation
2. Read **Sumers et al. (2023)** [#27] — CoALA connects neuroscience to AI agents
3. Study **Packer et al. (2023)** [#28] — MemGPT/Letta for practical implementation ideas
4. Review **Maharana et al. (2024)** [#30] — LoCoMo benchmark for evaluation
5. Examine **Jaiswal et al. (2025)** [#32-33] — Metacognition's mathematical framework
6. Understand **Hopfield (1982)** [#16] and **Ramsauer et al. (2020)** [#17] — Attractor memory and Transformer connection
7. Read **Church et al. (2012)** [#37] and **Erlich & Zielinski (2017)** [#39] — DNA storage foundations
8. Explore **Kirkpatrick et al. (2017)** [#18] — EWC for catastrophic forgetting prevention
